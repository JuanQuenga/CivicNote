CivicNote is an independent civic information app. It is not affiliated with any government agency or political campaign, and it does not require an account.

The app lets users follow public-interest topics (for example, local data-center proposals or surveillance-camera contracts) and receive push alerts before public hearings, votes, and comment deadlines. Every alert links to primary sources (official agendas, filings, government pages).

No login is needed to review the app. Notifications are optional and requested only after an explicit user action during onboarding or in Settings.
